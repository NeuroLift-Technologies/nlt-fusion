# Water Assets

Imported free UE5 water assets from Epic Fab:

| Asset | Fab Listing |
|-------|-------------|
| Stylized Water (rivers, waterfalls) | https://fab.com/listings/f2bdf5fa-bf17-4460-ab45-a16d5959d866 |
| Water Sound Samples (creek, rain, underwater) | https://fab.com/listings/371e46df-c3f7-4e01-9941-2ee9fc72fc20 |

## Usage

Water bodies for outdoor open-world areas (lakeside camp, river paths between
buildings). The `Stylized Water` system is compatible with UE 5 Lumen/Nanite.

## Integration Points

- `NLTAtmosphereSubsystem` — water reflection capture updates
- `Audio/Soundscape/` — water SFX data assets
