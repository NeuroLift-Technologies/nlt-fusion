NeuroLift Technologies · Simulation Environment

An AI habitat where agents live, perceive, act, and learn.

No human plays this world. AI Avatars carry ADHD traits and AI Aides support them; an RRT Advocate watches for crisis. Humans only observe, through a spectator viewer.

Below is the world itself: procedural terrain, water, sky, vegetation, and a settlement, with residents walking articulated bodies through it. This page is the single-file projection of the authoritative simulation — Fusion owns semantic reality; this owns physical reality.
