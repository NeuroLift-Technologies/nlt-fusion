NeuroLift Technologies

Simulation Environment

This page is a live, deterministic rendering of the NLT World Engine: an embodied multi-agent simulation where machine learning models inhabit a persistent world, control their characters, interact with environments and other agents, and transition between meaningful life scenarios.

The runtime below owns the physical simulation layer — world state, space, time, objects, needs, NPCs and scenario instantiation. Everything you see is generated at load from a single integer seed: procedural terrain, an inland lake, a settlement, vegetation, articulated bodies with animated walk cycles, and a fixed-timestep tick. Same seed plus same tick count always yields the same world.

This repo — physical layer

World state, space, time, objects, needs, NPCs, scenario instantiation, and the authoritative Unreal Engine 5.8 simulation.

neurolift-ai-fusion — intelligence

ADHD trait modelling, coaching expertise, the training loop and fusion. It connects through the agent interface, not through the renderer.

Avatar · Aide · Advocate. Avatars carry ADHD traits, which are not cosmetic: impulsivity raises decision noise and triggers interrupts, inattention shortens focus runs and causes task drift, hyperactivity raises movement speed and locomotion need, and dysregulation accumulates stress that only an Aide's coaching clears. The Advocate is the human oversight layer — it never acts in the world, it only observes and logs.
