## Notes

- All assets are free for UE5 projects (verify license on Fab before commercial use)
- Nanite/Lumen compatible unless otherwise noted
- Import into `WorldEngine/Content/` folder in the VM
- The WorldEngine project already has MassCrowd, PCG, and Learning Agents enabled — pair City Sample crowds with MassEntity for large-scale AI residents
